How to Play

Everything you need to know before drafting your six.

I

The Draft

You draft 6 leaders. Every round presents 5 random choices, and you select one.

You get one respin per attempt. A respin replaces all five current choices. Anyone already shown during that attempt cannot reappear.

II

Scouted Ratings

Every leader has six ratings. The exact values are hidden; you only see the ten-point band they fall within, such as 60–70 or 90–100.

Military
Command, force projection and battlefield capability.
Logistics
Moving people, supplies and resources across difficult campaigns.
Economy
Financing and sustaining a long global campaign.
Diplomacy
Coalitions, negotiation, legitimacy and political leverage.
Administration
State capacity, organization and maintaining control.
Adaptability
Responding to unfamiliar conditions and unexpected problems.
III

How Your Squad Is Scored

Your six leaders combine into one hidden team profile. The strongest leaders in a category matter most, but depth across the whole squad still contributes.

Chemistry matters. Diverse strengths, good administration, logistics and diplomacy can improve the team. Overloading the squad with the same kind of leader can hurt it.

Some traits also give small situational bonuses or penalties.

IV

The 193 Countries

After you lock the squad, it is tested against all 193 UN member states.

Each country has hidden challenge requirements across all six stats, plus two areas that matter more for that matchup. Larger, geographically difficult or especially demanding profiles tend to require stronger squads.

These values are an abstract game system, not a claim about real-world military or political strength.

V

Winning a Country

The game compares your team profile with that country's hidden requirements. Strong performance in the country's two focus areas receives extra weight.

A small amount of campaign uncertainty is applied, so borderline matchups can occasionally swing either way. If your final matchup score clears the hidden threshold, you earn a win.

Example campaign 187–6 187 countries conquered · 6 defeats
VI

The Goal

Your record is the number of country matchups won and lost. 193–0 is the perfect run.

A high-180s run is excellent. Reaching the 190s means the squad is extremely strong. Going 193–0 requires an exceptional draft with very few weaknesses.

VII

Packs & Rarity

The pool contains 263 leaders across six themed packs: Conquerors & Generals, Presidents & Prime Ministers, Infamous Leaders, Current Leaders, Kings & Queens, and Fictional & Legendary.

For historical and current figures, rarity now reflects overall six-stat strength as well as pull frequency. The pool is ranked into Common, Uncommon, Rare and Legendary power bands. Each choice independently rolls 60% Common, 24% Uncommon, 10% Rare, 5% Legendary and 1% Mythic.

VIII

Randomness

Each card slot rolls a rarity first, then chooses a person uniformly from that rarity. If someone appeared in your immediately previous attempt, the game avoids them whenever another card of that rarity is available.

There are 80 Common, 65 Uncommon, 53 Rare, 25 Legendary and 20 Mythic cards. The Fictional & Legendary pack is the exception to the power rule: its cards are always Mythic and can range from disastrous to absurdly powerful.
